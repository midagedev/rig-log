1The 3090's 20.0 GB of expert layers is worth 2.7 % of decode on the mean of ten runs and 0.3 % on the warm rows, against the 3–7 % the per-layer figures predicted.
2With one card the A6000 fills to 47 260 of 49 140 MiB. The work does not move to the other card — it moves to the host, which absorbs it.
3Placement moves the numerics, so the two arms answer with different text and the draft is right a different share of the time — 50.6 % against 59.6 % on one prompt. That effect is as big as the memory-bandwidth one, so this run bounds the cost without separating them.
