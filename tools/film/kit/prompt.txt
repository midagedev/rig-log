Make index.html: an original brick-breaker game as one self-contained page (canvas, inline JS and CSS, no other files, nothing from the network). A paddle at the bottom, a ball, and a wall of at least 6 rows by 10 columns of coloured bricks at the top; the ball bounces off the walls, the paddle and the bricks, a brick breaks when hit, and the angle off the paddle depends on where the ball lands on it. Show the score and lives at the top. Two power-ups drop from some broken bricks: a wider paddle and a second ball. The arrow keys or the mouse move the paddle; until the first key or mouse move, a demo mode plays by itself (the paddle follows the ball, never misses), so the game is in motion from the first frame. It fills a 1280x720 window. Check it with your eyes: `shot index.html 0`, `shot index.html 3` and `shot index.html 8` save 1280x720 screenshots at those seconds and print the page's console errors; look at them with read_image and fix what does not match (bricks breaking over time, the ball moving, no errors).
