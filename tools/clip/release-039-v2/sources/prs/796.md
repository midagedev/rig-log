# #796 metal: tensor-op GEMMs on the M5's neural accelerators (Metal 4 matmul2d) (@midagedev, OPEN)

The prompt pass's GEMMs on the M5's neural accelerators (Metal 4 tensor ops). Part of #593.

On the contributor's machines (#593) bloomery grows 1.25–1.29× from the M3 Ultra to the M5 Ultra, llama.cpp 2.0–2.1× and mlx-lm 2.2–3.1×. Our GEMMs multiply with `simdgroup_matrix` only, so they do not reach the M5's matrix units. MLX reaches them through its NAX kernels, llama.cpp through its tensor `mul_mm`.

- `kernels/gemm_tensor.metal`: the dense quantized GEMM (Q4_K, Q6_K, A4; f16 and f32 operands; store, residual, segmented) and the routed grouped GEMM (pair and plain) on `mpp::tensor_ops::matmul2d`, under the same host names, buffers and grid as today's. Design: MLX's NAX quantized matmul (weights dequantized to threadgroup memory, a 64×64 tile over 2×2 SIMD groups, the weight operand transposed). It stages the activations too (the f16 cast raises the activation fault), keeps relaxed precision off, and runs routed ops 16 places high. Written anew; no MLX code copied (THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES names the design).
- The shared GEMM ABI (geometry, argument blocks, `pair_swiglu`) moves to `kernels/gemm_shared.metal`. Today's kernels are unchanged.
- `MatrixUnits` (`library.rs`) is the one rule: Metal 4 and an Apple GPU generation ≥ 17 (≥ 18 for the phone class), MLX's `is_nax_available`. Such a device compiles the tensor source at language 4.0 beside the 3.2 library, and the GEMM pipelines come from it. Every other device keeps today's kernels. No flag.
- `metal_gemm_time` times the tensor arms beside today's (dense shapes and `--routed`), and its header prints the rule's verdict. `generate_metal` prints `gemm_path=`.

Bits: the tensor op's sum order is the hardware's, so the new kernels are held to a band for any order of the K products (accumulation term K instead of K/8 + 8; derivation in `clauses_gemm.rs`), against the same f64 oracle. Every tensor clause runs the kernels forced, on any Metal 4 device. The M1 Pro and the M4 Pro have no matrix units but run the tensor ops correctly, so the clauses run here.
