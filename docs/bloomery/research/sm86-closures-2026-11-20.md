## 1. Decomposition on paper (the one question: which closures flip, and what term decides)

**Decode step, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B Q4_K_M whole on the card** (the only model with both an A6000 and a 5090 number):

| Term | A6000 | RTX 5090 |
|---|---|---|
| Bytes a step | 2061.4 MB (B:`research/sparkdec.md:43`) | same |
| Measured tok/s → step | 203.58 → 4.912 ms (depth 6, B:`research/sparkdec.md:513-516`) | 345.2 → 2.897 ms (juliankang4, ~4K prompt, 0.3.0, B:`numbers.md:201`; 344.1 at 0.3.10, `numbers.md:187-188`) |
| Byte time at plain-read fraction | 579–701 GB/s (R:`plan.md:72`) → 3.56–2.94 ms [유도 2061.4/579, /701] | 75–91 % of 1.79 TB/s → 1.54–1.27 ms [유도 2061.4/(0.75·1790), /(0.91·1790)]; at 100 % 1.15 ms |
| **Non-byte time** (step − bytes) | 1.35–1.97 ms = 27–40 % | **1.36–1.63 ms = 47–56 %** |
| Node gap, 520 nodes (B:`research/sparkdec.md:239`) × c_node | 520 × 0.852 = 0.44 ms = 9.0 % | 520 × (0.553 GB10 … 0.852 A6000) = 0.29–0.44 ms = **10–15 %** [c_node on the 5090 is unmeasured] |

Reading: the byte term fell 2.3x, the non-byte term did not move (about 1.4–1.6 ms both). On the 5090 roughly half the decode step is launch tails, latency chains and gemvs below the plain-read rate. Every closure whose reason was "that term is a small share of the A6000 step" or "kernel is near ~700 GB/s" is therefore stale in magnitude (not necessarily in sign). **Conditions mismatch**: depth 6 (A6000) vs ~4K (5090), different commits; the 5090 attention term at 4K depth makes the 5090 non-byte estimate an upper bound. The decisive term is one table: per-kernel GB/s and c_node on the 5090 at depth 6 (see M0).

**Resource timeline, one decode step (whole-on-card path)**: card SMs/DRAM 2.90 ms; PCIe: token id in, argmax out, a few KB → ~0.00x ms; host CPU: graph replay, ~0 on the critical path; host DRAM 0; NVMe 0. Wall = the card alone (one resource's max). Flow levers available: asynchrony *inside* the card (PDL: kernel n+1's prologue and first weight reads under kernel n's tail); bulk and SIMT are already in (graph, grids over rows). No host-side overlap exists to buy.

**Timeline, one prefill layer-batch (MoE GEMM kernels)**: card only; the wall is the kernel sum plus wave tails. Wave arithmetic uses the SM count (84 → 170).
